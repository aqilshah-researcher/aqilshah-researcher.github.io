About me

Data analyst and monitoring, evaluation and learning expert, now based in Rockville, Maryland.

I turn field and program data into dashboards, evaluations and reports that people use to make decisions.

For more than 10 years, I have built data systems and analyzed data for programs funded by USAID, the UN, the World Bank, FCDO, the EU and SIDA. Most of my field work has been in Afghanistan, often as part of multi-country programs such as CARE's Every Voice Counts and the Cordaid-led Just Future alliance.

None of this was solo work. At every organization I worked under experienced leaders, such as chiefs of party, CEOs, country directors and heads of programs, who guided the work and reviewed what we produced. I also worked alongside research and data colleagues and field teams who carried much of the work with me.

My work covers the whole data cycle. I design survey tools and sampling plans, train and supervise field teams, build cleaning and validation pipelines in Python, R and SQL, and turn the results into Power BI and Tableau dashboards and clear evaluation reports. Along the way I have contributed to 55+ evaluation and third-party monitoring assignments, managed 40,000+ field interviews and authored 100+ analytical reports.

I have worked across health, education, livelihoods and food security, workforce development, women's economic empowerment, governance and peacebuilding, and mine action, with firms and organizations such as MSI, Adroit Associates, TAGHEER, Itad and Search for Common Ground. I work in English, Dari and Pashto, which lets me train local teams and listen to communities directly.

Now based in Rockville, Maryland, I am looking for data, BI and MEL roles in health, workforce development, nonprofit and government work, in the DC area or remote, where I can lead and mentor teams as well as the data.
