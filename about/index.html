About me

Data analyst and monitoring, evaluation and learning expert, now based in Rockville, Maryland.

I turn field and program data into dashboards, evaluations and reports that people use to make decisions.

For more than 10 years I have built data systems and led analysis for programs funded by USAID, the UN, the World Bank, FCDO, the EU and SIDA, mostly in Afghanistan and often as part of multi-country programs. I am now looking for data, BI and MEL roles in health, workforce development, nonprofit and government work, in the DC area or remote.

My story

From field data to decisions

I started in health facility data management, then coordinated regional monitoring for USAID, DOD and UNDP projects. Since then I have led TPM data systems for USAID at MSI, Power BI dashboards for 17+ donor portfolios at TAGHEER, and evaluations and research at Adroit Associates and Search for Common Ground.

I hold an MBA and certifications in Power BI (PL-300), SQL (Oracle) and MEAL DPro.
