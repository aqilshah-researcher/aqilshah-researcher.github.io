Project Overview

AQBE is implemented by IRC in partnership with Save the Children, ABRAAR, The Liaison Office (TLO), WADAN, COAR, and OCCD. The program works with students, teachers, community members, and Ministry of Education representatives to design and scale education interventions so that Afghan children — particularly girls, displaced, disadvantaged, and rural children — have improved safe access to quality learning opportunities.

My role focused on building and maintaining the MEL data infrastructure: configuring CommCare digital data collection tools, designing Power BI dashboards for real-time monitoring, supporting data quality assurance, and producing analytical reports for USAID and program leadership.

Evaluation & Assessment Schedule

AQBE uses a rigorous multi-level assessment system supported by IRC's Airbel Impact Lab, combining baseline/midline/endline household surveys with longitudinal CBE transition evaluations:

Y1, Y3, Y5
Household Assessments using IRC's AMAL toolkit — EGRA, EGMA, SEL, demographics
Y3, Y5
Cohort-Based Longitudinal Transition Evaluations — 6 months post-CBE transition
Annual
Rapid Education Risk Analyses (RERA) — conflict-sensitive, adaptive management
Quarterly
Pause-and-Reflect Learning Events — PMU + partners + USAID review sessions
