AKF · Ministry of Education · TAGHEER · 2021

Teacher training needs assessment

Field assessment for the Aga Khan Foundation and the Ministry of Education to choose the topics for digital training videos for teachers, principals and academic supervisors under the Afghanistan Quality Learning program.

Client
Aga Khan Foundation Afghanistan
Partner
Ministry of Education (GDAS)
Firm
TAGHEER Mosbat Consultancy
Period
Feb – Mar 2021
My role
Data Analyst: tools, field research, analysis

The assignment

A review of more than 30 ministry documents produced 100 candidate topics. Key informant interviews in five Kabul schools at high, medium and low performance levels, plus group discussions with partners and ministry staff, scored and ranked them. The result was 72 validated topics: 36 for teachers, 18 for principals and 18 for academic supervisors, in Dari and Pashto.

My contribution to the team's work

This was a team effort. My part included:

  • Designed the interview guides with topic scoring, ranking and open questions for new topics.
  • Took part in the field interviews and group discussions.
  • Worked with the evaluation team on the scoring and ranking that narrowed 100 candidate topics to 72, and agreed them with AKF and the ministry.

Whom I worked with

I reported to the CEO and founder, who guided the work. Team members working with me supported delivery and reported back through me.
