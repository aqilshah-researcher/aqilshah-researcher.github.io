Third-party monitoring data system for USAID programs

As Senior Data Systems & M&E Advisor at MSI, I ran the data side of a large third-party monitoring (TPM) contract: thousands of site visits a year across health, education, agriculture and governance programs, reported to USAID mission leadership through Power BI.

The assignment

USAID used third-party monitors to verify that activities happened as reported: distributions, trainings, health facility services, school activities and grants. Each visit produced a structured form, photos and GPS points, and any problem had to reach USAID quickly as a yellow or red flag.

I was also TPM Assignment Manager for specific awards, including the AFIAT (MSH) and UHI (Jhpiego) health facility projects, AVCL and AVCP (DAI), AMP and ASP.

What I did

  • Built Python (pandas, NumPy) pipelines that cut monthly processing from about 20 analyst hours to 8, around 60 percent less.
  • Designed the data flow from Survey123 field forms, through Python cleaning and validation, to Power BI dashboards with row-level security.
  • Defined the flag rules (yellow and red) and the KPIs: planned, completed, partner postponed or canceled, and reporting time.
  • Used sampling to calculate health facility caseloads for AFIAT and UHI and turned them into monthly and weekly visit schedules.
  • Built baseline, endline and TPM instruments in Survey123 for 10 projects and trained 100+ MEAL officers and partner staff.

How the data flowed

  1. 1Monitors submit visit forms in Survey123 (with GPS and photos)
  2. 2Python cleans names, dates and statuses, removes duplicates and logs QA issues
  3. 3Clean tables load into a star-schema model
  4. 4Power BI shows KPIs, maps and flags, with row-level security by award
  5. 5Flags go to USAID and implementing partners for follow-up
