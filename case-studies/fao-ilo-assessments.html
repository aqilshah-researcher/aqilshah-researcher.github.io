FAO · ILO · Adroit Associates · 2021–2022

Food security TPM and labour assessments

As Senior Research, Data & M&E Project Manager at Adroit Associates, I managed large field data operations for UN agencies, including FAO emergency food security monitoring and ILO assessments in Balkh.

The assignment

Assignments included third-party monitoring of FAO's emergency food security response, ILO assessments in Balkh (workplace safety in small businesses trained under a decent-jobs project, and hazardous work for children), and a WASH and health care needs assessment for DAARTT.

What I did

  • Managed data collection for more than 40,000 field interviews, with automated validation in R.
  • Built R pipelines for cleaning, merging and reporting across evaluations and TPM exercises.
  • Coded about 124 interview transcripts a month in ATLAS.ti with deductive and inductive themes.
  • Managed budgets, workplans and vendors to meet deadlines.
