FAO · ILO · Adroit Associates · 2021–2022

Food security TPM and labour assessments

As Senior Research, Data & M&E Project Manager at Adroit Associates, I managed large field data operations for UN agencies under the guidance of the CEO and the President, with the research and data teams, including FAO emergency food security monitoring and ILO assessments in Balkh.

My role

The assignment

Assignments included third-party monitoring of FAO's emergency food security response, two ILO assessments in Balkh delivered by Adroit Associates (occupational safety and health good practices in small businesses trained under the Road to Decent Jobs for All Afghans project, and hazardous work for children), and a WASH and health care needs assessment for DAARTT.

My contribution to the team's work

This was a team effort. My part included:

  • Managed data collection for more than 40,000 field interviews with the field and data teams, with automated validation in R.
  • Built R pipelines with the data team for cleaning, merging and reporting across evaluations and TPM exercises.
  • Coded about 124 interview transcripts a month in ATLAS.ti with deductive and inductive themes.
  • Managed budgets, workplans and vendors to meet deadlines, with guidance from the CEO and the President.

Whom I worked with

I worked under the guidance of the CEO and the President of the company, together with colleagues on the research and data teams.
