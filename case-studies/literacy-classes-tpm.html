UNESCO · Grant Thornton Afghanistan · 2023–2024

Third-party monitoring of basic literacy classes

Third-party monitoring of UNESCO's Basic General Literacy (BGL) classes, spot checks and an assessment of implementing partners. As the data expert, I built and ran the digital data collection system.

Client
UNESCO
Firm
Grant Thornton Afghanistan
Period
Nov 2023 – Jan 2024
Coverage
Classes in 11 provinces · 34 focus groups
My role
Data expert (Digital Data Collection, KoBo Toolbox Consultant)

The assignment

Field teams visited literacy classes run by several implementing partners, checked them against a standard checklist, tested learners and held focus groups with village literacy committees, facilitators, elders and learners. Female enumerators visited female classes.

What I did

  • Reviewed the class monitoring tool and learner assessment question bank and gave feedback.
  • Built the forms in KoBo Toolbox with skip logic, validation rules, GPS location, live photos and enumerator IDs, then tested them.
  • Trained enumerators and supervisors on KoBo Collect, including offline collection, and solved issues during fieldwork.
  • Reviewed data validity and accuracy daily and made sure the data stayed complete and consistent, with daily backups and a dedicated UNESCO account for review.

How the data flowed

  1. 1Tool review and KoBo form build
  2. 2Enumerator training on KoBo Collect
  3. 3Class visits with GPS, photos and learner tests (offline when needed)
  4. 4Daily review of submissions and feedback to enumerators
  5. 5Clean dataset and partner-level results for the report
