Radio-based literacy and alternative learning: final evaluation

Final evaluation of a UNESCO program that used radio lessons and self-study materials to reach out-of-school adolescents and adults with literacy, secondary learning and psychosocial support. I was the Data Analyst and Reporting Specialist.

Client
UNESCO Kabul Office
Firm
Grant Thornton Afghanistan (through Kudos Afghanistan)
Period
Feb – Apr 2025
Coverage
Balkh, Helmand, Herat, Kandahar, Nangarhar
My role
Data Analyst & Reporting Specialist

The assignment

The program targeted around 40,000 out-of-school adolescent girls and boys and 4,600 adults with limited literacy. The evaluation used the OECD/DAC criteria and a mixed-methods design: on-site and remote learner surveys, a short learning assessment, key informant interviews and observation.

The evaluation team lead acknowledged my work in cleaning and analyzing the field data and preparing the report.

What I did

  • Cleaned and validated data from 400+ learner and stakeholder interviews across five provinces.
  • Analyzed the learner assessment (reading, writing, numeracy items) and survey results by province and sex.
  • Coded and summarized qualitative interviews alongside the survey results.
  • Prepared the evaluation report, tables and charts against the OECD/DAC criteria.

How the data flowed

  1. 1Survey and assessment data from field teams
  2. 2Back-checks and cleaning
  3. 3Item and domain scores, disaggregation by province and sex
  4. 4Qualitative coding and triangulation
