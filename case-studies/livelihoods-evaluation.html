Livelihoods and resilience program: quantitative final evaluation

I led the quantitative side of the final evaluation of a BMZ-funded nexus program that combined cash-for-work, cash transfers, livestock and agricultural support, kitchen gardens and solar-powered water systems for drought- and conflict-affected households.

Donor
BMZ (Germany)
Firm
Premium Performance Consulting
Period
Sep 2024 – Jan 2025
Coverage
6 provinces · 21,500+ households reached
My role
Quantitative Data Analysis Consultant

The assignment

The program aimed to help vulnerable households meet basic needs without harmful coping, and to build longer-term resilience through irrigation, livestock, training and water infrastructure. The evaluation compared results with baseline and midline values and judged the program against the OECD/DAC criteria.

What I did

  • Built the evaluation framework: indicator definitions, sampling strategy and data collection tools.
  • Calculated the standard food security indicators: Food Consumption Score, Reduced Coping Strategies Index and Minimum Dietary Diversity for Women.
  • Ran disaggregated analysis by province, intervention and sex of household head, with descriptive and inferential statistics.
  • Combined the household survey with key informant interviews, focus groups and site observation of irrigation and solar water systems.
  • Wrote the data sections of the final report with practical recommendations.

How the data flowed

  1. 1Household survey and observation checklist in KoBo Toolbox
  2. 2Cleaning and indicator calculation (FCS, rCSI, MDD-W)
  3. 3Comparison with baseline and midline, tests of change
  4. 4Disaggregation by province, intervention and household head
  5. 5Report tables, charts and recommendations
