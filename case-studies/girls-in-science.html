Girls in Science project: endline evaluation

Endline evaluation of the Aga Khan Foundation's Girls in Science pilot, which ran science clubs for adolescent girls in 13 government schools. I was the Data Analyst on the evaluation team.

Client
Aga Khan Foundation Afghanistan
Funder
Vitol Foundation
Firm
TAGHEER Mosbat Consultancy
Coverage
13 schools · 473 stakeholders
My role
Data Analyst

The assignment

The pilot (2018 to 2021) aimed to give girls equitable access to quality science education and to influence education policy. The evaluation compared results with the logframe and the OECD/DAC criteria, using surveys of students, parents and teachers, interviews with officials and school observation.

What I did

  • Helped the data collectors understand the tools before fieldwork.
  • Cleaned and analyzed the quantitative and qualitative data from 473 students, parents, teachers and officials.
  • Prepared clean, analyzed datasets and tables for the evaluation lead and the report.

How the data flowed

  1. 1Tool orientation for data collectors
  2. 2Surveys, interviews and school observation
  3. 3Cleaning and analysis
  4. 4Analysis datasets for reporting
