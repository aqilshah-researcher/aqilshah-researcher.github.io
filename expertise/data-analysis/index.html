Expertise 01

Data analysis, ETL & dashboards

I design the full flow of program data, from the form on a phone in the field to the dashboard a donor opens on Monday morning.

Data pipelinesResultsDashboardsTools

Clean, timely and trusted data is what lets a program team act. I build the pipelines and dashboards that make that happen.

My work spans digital data collection, automated cleaning and validation in Python and R, SQL and dbt models, and Power BI and Tableau dashboards with row-level security for donors and program teams.

Data pipelines

How my data pipelines work

From field form to decision

Step 1

Collect

KoBo Toolbox, Survey123, SurveyCTO, ODK and DHIS2 forms with built-in checks.

Step 2

Clean & validate

Python and R scripts flag duplicates, outliers, GPS and skip-logic errors.

Step 3

Model

SQL and dbt models join visits, sites and indicators into tidy tables.

Step 4

Visualize

Power BI (DAX, row-level security) and Tableau dashboards for each audience.

Step 5

Act

Red and yellow flags and monthly reports reach donors and program teams.
