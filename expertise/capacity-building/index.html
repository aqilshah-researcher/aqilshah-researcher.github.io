Other trainings I have delivered

Alongside these guides, I have designed and led many in-person trainings. They include enumerator and supervisor trainings before evaluations and surveys, and internal evaluation team trainings. I also ran monitoring and evaluation workshops for civil society partners, capacity building for local sub-grantees with pre- and post-tests, and community accountability workshops. I delivered them in English, Dari and Pashto. The materials belong to the organizations I delivered them for, and I am happy to describe them in more detail on request.
