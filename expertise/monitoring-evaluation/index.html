Expertise 02

Monitoring and evaluation

Baseline, midline and endline evaluations, third-party monitoring and learning reviews for USAID, FCDO, UNESCO, ILO, FAO, SIDA and BMZ-funded programs.

My approachTPM, evaluations and assessmentsSite visit mapsIn the field

Good evaluation answers the questions decision makers actually have, with evidence they can trust.

I have contributed to 55+ evaluation and third-party monitoring assignments and authored 100+ analytical reports, with field teams collecting 40,000+ interviews.

My approach

How I run monitoring and evaluation

  • Evaluation design. OECD/DAC criteria, theory of change, mixed methods and sampling plans
  • Third-party monitoring. site visit planning, verification, red and yellow flags and reporting to donors
  • MEL frameworks. logframes, indicators and PIRS, data quality assessments
  • Quantitative research. surveys with digital data collection tools, analyzed in SPSS, Stata, R, Python or any other tool the client prefers
  • Qualitative research. KIIs, FGDs and outcome harvesting, coded in ATLAS.ti and NVivo
  • Learning. findings turned into clear recommendations, briefs and dashboards
