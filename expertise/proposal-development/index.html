Proposal development

At Adroit Associates and Kudos Afghanistan I drafted more than 40 technical proposals, expressions of interest and concept notes, refined with review from leadership and colleagues.

My processAssignment typesClients

A strong proposal shows the client you understand their questions and have a realistic plan to answer them.

My process

How I build a proposal

Step 1

Read the ToR

Unpack the questions, scope, deliverables and scoring criteria.

Step 2

Design the method

Evaluation design, sampling, tools and a data quality plan.

Step 3

Plan the work

Workplan, team, field logistics, risk and ethics plans.

Step 4

Write and review

I draft the proposal, share it with the CEO, the President and colleagues for review, and refine it before submission.
