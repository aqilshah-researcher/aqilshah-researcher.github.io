Project Overview

The Afghanistan Quality Learning (AQL) program sought to improve the quality and relevance of education in rural areas by supporting academic supervision, teacher development, and improved learning environments. TAGHEER was engaged by AKF to conduct a comprehensive field-based needs assessment to identify and validate the most relevant topics for digital video-based learning resources in both Dari and Pashto languages.

The assessment involved literature review of MoE/GDAS curriculum documents, structured KIIs with three categories of education professionals, group discussions with AQLC partners and MoE staff, and a rigorous validation process to finalize 72 priority topics across Teachers (36), Principals (18), and Academic Supervisors (18).

Assessment Process
01
Literature Review
Reviewed 30+ GDAS/MoE documents from Feb 21–29, 2021. Identified 100 candidate topics across all 3 beneficiary categories.
02
Tool Development
Designed interview guides in consultation with MoE/GDAS staff. Included topic scoring, priority ranking, and open-ended questions for new topic discovery.
03
Field Assessment
Conducted 32 KIIs across 5 Kabul schools (High, Medium, Low levels) from March 2–18, 2021. Plus group discussions with AQLC partners and GDAS/MoE staff.
04
Data Analysis
Scored and ranked 100 topics using combined quantitative scoring from KIIs and group discussions. Finalized 72 priority topics agreed with AKF and MoE/GDAS.
