About this project

Modeled on an ILO-funded assessment by Adroit Associates (2022) of occupational safety and health good practices in small and medium businesses in Balkh that had been trained under the Road to Decent Jobs for All Afghans project. The real assessment used semi-structured interviews and checklist-based observation with before and after comparisons (passageway marking, lighting and ventilation, fire safety, first aid, supervision).

Method shown here

  • Each business is observed twice, so every checklist item is a paired yes/no outcome.
  • McNemar's exact test per item shows which changes are larger than chance.
  • A business-level compliance score shows whether gains are broad or driven by a few businesses.
