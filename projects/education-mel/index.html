About this project

Modeled on MEL data support for a USAID-funded basic education activity: CommCare data collection, an indicator tracker against life-of-activity targets, quarterly data quality checks and Power BI dashboards for program and donor reporting.

Method shown here

  • Outputs are cumulative and compared to cumulative targets; outcomes are percentages compared to their target rate.
  • Status rule: on track at 90% or more, watch at 75 to 89%, off track below 75%.
  • Every record can carry a data quality flag (late, missing disaggregation, out of range) for follow-up before reporting.
