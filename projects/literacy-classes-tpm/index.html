About this project

Modeled on the third-party monitoring of a UNESCO basic general literacy program by Grant Thornton Afghanistan (2023 to 2024), where I was the data expert: I reviewed the tools, built the forms in KoBo Toolbox, tested them, trained enumerators on KoBo Collect, solved field issues and reviewed data validity and integrity. Teams visited classes in 11 provinces, assessed learners and held 34 focus group discussions.

Method shown here

  • Six-item class checklist and a spot-check result for every class, including ghost classes that did not exist on the day.
  • Attendance on the visit day against enrollment.
  • Five learners per class tested on letters, words, sentences and numbers.
  • A ranked scorecard per implementing partner.
