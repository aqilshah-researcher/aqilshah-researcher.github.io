About this project

Modeled on the training needs assessment I worked on as Data Analyst at TAGHEER for the Aga Khan Foundation and the Ministry of Education (2021): a literature review produced 100 candidate topics, key informant interviews in five Kabul schools and group discussions scored them, and 72 priority topics were validated for digital teacher-training content in Dari and Pashto.

Method shown here

  • Weighted score per topic: 0.6 × mean relevance + 0.4 × mean urgency.
  • Agreement check: topics where informants disagree (SD of relevance 1.2 or more) are not validated.
  • Quota per group: the highest-scoring agreed topics fill 36 / 18 / 18 places.
