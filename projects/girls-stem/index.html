About this project

Modeled on the endline evaluation of a girls' science education pilot in 13 government schools, where I was the Data Analyst (TAGHEER, 2021): I trained the data collection team on the tools, then cleaned and analyzed the quantitative and qualitative data from 473 students, parents, teachers and officials and prepared the analysis datasets for the report.

Method shown here

  • Paired t-test on baseline and endline marks for the same students.
  • Club vs non-club: Welch t-test on gains with Cohen's d, then an OLS model with grade and school fixed effects and robust standard errors, so the club effect is not just a school effect.
  • Attitudes: top-2-box on five-point Likert items.
  • The analysis is written in Python and again as a Stata do-file.
