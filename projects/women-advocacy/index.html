About this project

Modeled on the final evaluation I worked on in 2025, reporting to the Country Director, as Data Analytics and Lead Evaluation Consultant for Search for Common Ground, of a SIDA-funded program supporting women professionals in health, media and business. The real report is confidential, so this page shows only the method with simulated data and generic hub names.

Method shown here

  • Agency index: mean of four Likert items (confidence, voice in decisions, network support, advocacy skills), compared baseline to endline with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test.
  • Dose-response: Spearman correlation between cluster sessions attended and gain.
  • Outcome harvesting: each advocacy action is logged with type, outcome and whether a second source verified it.
