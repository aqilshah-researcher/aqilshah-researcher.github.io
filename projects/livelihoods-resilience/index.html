About this project

Modeled on the quantitative final evaluation I worked on, under the team's lead researcher, as Quantitative Data Analysis Consultant for Premium Performance Consulting (2024 to 2025): a BMZ-funded livelihoods and resilience program in six provinces covering cash-for-work, unconditional cash transfers, livestock support, kitchen gardens and solar-powered water systems for more than 21,500 vulnerable households. I built the indicator framework, sampling and tools, and ran the disaggregated analysis.

Method shown here

  • Food Consumption Score (FCS): weighted days of eight food groups over 7 days, grouped Poor / Borderline / Acceptable using the WFP thresholds.
  • Reduced Coping Strategies Index (rCSI): five weighted coping behaviours; 19+ is treated as high coping.
  • MDD-W: share of women 15 to 49 eating at least 5 of 10 food groups.
  • Tests: Welch t-test and chi-square for baseline vs endline, and a logistic regression of acceptable FCS on sex of household head, province, intervention and household size. The R script and Python results match row for row.
