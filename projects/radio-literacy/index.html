About this project

Modeled on two pieces of real work. First, the final evaluation of a radio-based literacy and alternative learning program for out-of-school adolescents and adults, where I served as Data Analyst and Reporting Specialist (Grant Thornton Afghanistan, 2025): I cleaned and analyzed 400+ learner and stakeholder interviews and prepared the evaluation report against the OECD/DAC criteria. Second, the KoBo Toolbox data system I built for a literacy TPM across 10 provinces (2023 to 2024), with skip logic, validation, GPS and daily QA.

Method shown here

  • Item analysis: percent correct (difficulty) and item-rest correlation (discrimination) for every test item.
  • Reliability: KR-20 for the full 12-item test.
  • Group differences: domain scores by province and sex, and a chi-square test of self-rated improvement by province.
  • The same steps are written twice: in Python and in SPSS syntax.
