About this project

Modeled on my work as Itad's in-country expert for the FCDO-funded Global Mine Action Programme 3 (GMAP3) in Afghanistan (2023 to 2024). I joined the international MEL visit to clearance sites and risk education sessions, co-led key informant interviews and focus groups, and turned the notes into coded evidence for the visit report, the formative evaluation and the pause-and-reflect sessions.

Method shown here

  • Monthly outputs: area cleared, items destroyed, risk education reach split by girls, boys, women and men.
  • Consultation log: every KII and FGD with stakeholder group and number of women and men.
  • Evidence matrix: coded references by theme and OECD/DAC criterion, so thin evidence is easy to spot before the next visit.
